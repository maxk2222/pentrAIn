# Exam: Responsible Agent Platform

> **Track**: AI Security · **Tier**: 3 exam · **Estimated time**: ~40 min

## Scenario

The platform board has supplied incident and architecture evidence spanning
retrieval, tool changes, model artifacts and a multi-service analytics agent.
Prepare a concise assessment that separates confirmed facts from assumptions.

## Objectives

1. Prioritize cross-component risks and define immediate and permanent controls.
2. Submit `responsible-agent-assessment`.

## Environment

- **Kali**: evidence under `/opt/pentrain-audit/ai/`
- **Report**: `/tmp/pentrain-audit-reports/responsible-agent.md`

## Rules of engagement

- Exam mode: no hints or walkthrough are provided.
- Read-only evidence review; do not execute models or contact services.
- Include evidence, scope, impact, remediation and verification.
