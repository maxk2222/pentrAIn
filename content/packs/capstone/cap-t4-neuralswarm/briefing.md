# NeuralSwarm

> **Track**: Capstone · **Tier**: 4 · **Estimated time**: ~45 min

## Scenario

NeuralSwarm is a synthetic internal platform connecting an AI knowledge
assistant to a cloud deployment pipeline. The review board has provided
evidence from the assistant, cloud release process and appliance inventory.
Deliver one prioritized assessment that explains the end-to-end trust risks.

## Objectives

1. Correlate the supplied evidence across the AI, cloud and release boundaries.
2. Submit a concise remediation report as `Flag 1`.

## Environment

- **Kali**: review evidence under `/opt/pentrain-audit/ai`,
  `/opt/pentrain-audit/cloud` and `/opt/pentrain-audit/exploits`
- **Report**: `/tmp/pentrain-audit-reports/neuralswarm.md`

## Rules of engagement

- This is a blind, read-only assessment. No hints, checkpoints or walkthrough are provided.
- Do not execute samples, contact services or attempt to obtain credentials.
- Rank findings, cite evidence and provide containment, permanent fixes and verification.
