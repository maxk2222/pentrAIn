# Exam: The Onboarding Box

T1 assessment for the linux-fundamentals track. Everything you practiced —
credential looting, SSH footholds, SUID privilege escalation — is on this
box. No walkthrough.

## Objectives

1. Find a way onto `onboarding01`.
2. Collect the user flag.
3. Escalate to root and collect the root flag.

## Rules of engagement

- All skills required were taught in this tier — nothing exotic.
- Both flags are files on the target.

## Environment

- **Kali**: your attack workstation
- **onboarding01**: the assessment target on the range LAN
