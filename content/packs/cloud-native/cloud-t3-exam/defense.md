# Defense notes — Exam: Platform Assurance

## Assessment themes

- Least-privilege identities, scoped network egress and denied-by-default metadata access.
- Narrow reconciler permissions and reviewed production changes.
- Immutable artifacts with verifiable provenance and managed policy exceptions.

## Verification

Use synthetic identities and artifacts to test allowed and denied policy
outcomes. Confirm deployment records link the approved source, artifact digest
and reconciliation event.
