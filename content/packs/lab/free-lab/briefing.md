# Free Lab

A completely open Kali environment with a real internet uplink — no
objectives, no flags, no scope whitelist.

Use it for whatever you want: try tools against your own authorized
targets, follow an external tutorial, test a hypothesis, or just poke
around. The coach agent runs at the autonomy level you chose and drops
its scope restrictions here; the environment is disposable — reset
rebuilds it from the template.

You are still responsible for acting on targets you are authorized to
test.
