# Exam: The Leaky Portal

T1 assessment for the web-appsec track. A login form that never met a prepared
statement, and a deploy pipeline that never learned what `.git` in a webroot
means.

## Objectives

1. Map what `portal01` is serving — all of it, not just the landing page.
2. Beat the login and collect the portal flag.
3. Turn source disclosure into a shell and collect the deploy flag.

## Rules of engagement

- Two flags, two disciplines: web exploitation and source disclosure.
- Everything required was taught in this tier.

## Environment

- **Kali**: your attack workstation
- **portal01**: the assessment target on the range LAN
